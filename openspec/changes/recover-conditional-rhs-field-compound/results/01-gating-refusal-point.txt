## Reading the trace: where the RHS criterion stops short

* The copy is the `dup` at **BCI 21**, and its two copies are `ValueId(33)` (the value that stays
  under the read) and `ValueId(34)` (the value the read is called on). The read at **BCI 22** is the
  copy's own next instruction and its receiver is `ValueId(34)` — the ordinary proof's first two
  facts hold.
* `ValueId(33)` — the receiver the **write** is called on — is consumed by the `putfield` at
  **BCI 36**, and that instruction stands in the **join block** (`bci 35`): its use records are the
  join's own entry Phi (two Phi inputs at block 35) plus the store at BCI 36. The store is *not* in
  the copy's block (`bci 14`).
* `receiver_copy_at` reads the copy's consumers with `instructions.iter().skip(index + 1)` — the
  copy's **own block** only. The write's receiver is therefore never seen, `store` stays `None`, and
  the proof refuses at `let (Some((read_bci, read_receiver)), Some((store_bci, store_receiver))) =
  (read, store) else { return Ok(None) }`. That is the refusal point; the text the presentation
  carries for it is `duplicate_expression`'s fallback, `the copy at BCI 21 has no proved local
  assignment`.
* The right-hand side `x > 0` is the branch at **BCI 27** (`ifle 34`): its arms push `iconst_1`
  (BCI 30) and `iconst_0` (BCI 34) and the join's stack Phi is `ValueId(15)`, consumed once by the
  `iand` at BCI 35. The region pass **already proves exactly this region** — the trace's last line,
  `conditional region: branch=27 attempt=Proved(ConditionalValueProof { branch: bci 14, branch_bci:
  27, join: bci 35, phi: ValueId(15), when_true: ValueId(40), when_false: ValueId(41),
  consumer_bci: 35 })` — which is the sub-proof channel the certificate reuses read-only.
* The cascade diagnostic `the saved producer at BCI 22 has 3 consumers` is the deferred-binding
  plan counting the read's value (`ValueId(35)`): two uses are the join's entry Phi records and one
  is the real read at BCI 35. The Phi records are not evaluations, which is exactly what the
  concatenation rule's own cut already states for its chain (`crosses_a_proved_cut`).
